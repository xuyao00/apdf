# aPDF app icon

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, with a transparent background. The graphite tile and open paper book follow the app's greyscale palette. The generated image contains no lettering, so the aPDF capitalization is applied by the app and installer.

Source: `build/icon-master.png`. Distributable assets: `build/icon.png` and `build/icon.icns`. Generate all macOS sizes with `npm run icons:mac`.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production macOS application icon for APDF, a minimal PDF reader. Create one polished square app icon, straight-on, centered and isolated on a genuinely transparent background. A large dark graphite rounded-square tile fills about 84% of the square canvas, with generous transparent margin for the macOS Dock. In its center is a distinctive sculptural open book made of two simple thick ivory paper shapes meeting at a soft central V, with a small folded page corner that suggests a PDF document. Strong, instantly readable silhouette at 32 pixels. Restrained macOS material treatment: softly beveled tile edges, delicate monochrome lighting, shallow paper depth, and a very subtle shadow. Strictly achromatic palette: white, ivory-like neutral grey, charcoal, black, silver-grey; all RGB channels must be equal, absolutely no green, blue, warm color, or colored accent. Balanced geometric proportions and quiet modern character. No text, no letters, no numbers, no badge, no border outside the tile, no surrounding scenery, no perspective tilt, no watermark. Deliver a single icon, not a sheet of variants.

The application was named **aPDF** after generation; the image has no text and remains unchanged.
